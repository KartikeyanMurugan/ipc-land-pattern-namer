How to name SOT-23, SOT-23-5 and SOT-223 footprints

SOT names use pitch, lead span and height. The catch is the pitch: when some pins are missing, use the pin-to-pin grid pitch, not the larger spacing.

The formula

SOT + pitch P + lead span X height - pin count + density SOT95P280X145-5N = 0.95 mm pitch, 2.80 mm lead span, 1.45 mm max height, 5 pins

Step by step

  1. Pitch – e, the grid between neighbouring pin positions: 0.95 mm → 95P. SOT-223 is 2.30 mm → 230P.
  2. Lead span – E, nominal, lead tip to lead tip.
  3. Height – A maximum.
  4. Pin count – count the tab of a SOT-223 as a pin: -4.
  5. Pin assignment (optional) – transistors may add _BEC, _SGD or _213 before the pin count, for example SOT95P280X160_BEC-3N.

Common mistakes

Using e1On SOT-23 the outer pins are 1.90 mm apart (e1). The name still uses 0.95 mm (e).
Same name for different pinoutsA BJT and a MOSFET in SOT-23 share pads but not pin functions. Add _BEC / _SGD when your library keeps them separate.
Height from the wrong columnManufacturers differ (1.12, 1.30, 1.45 mm max). Use the part’s own datasheet, not a generic SOT-23 value.
