How to name SOIC, SOP, SSOP and TSSOP footprints

Gull-wing packages are named from pitch, lead span and height. Almost every wrong name comes from using the body width instead of the lead span.

The formula

SOIC127P + lead span X height - pin count + density SOIC127P600X175-8N = 1.27 mm pitch SOIC, 6.00 mm lead span, 1.75 mm max height, 8 pins
SOP + pitch P + lead span X height - pin count + density SOP65P640X120-20N = 0.65 mm pitch (TSSOP-20), 6.40 mm lead span, 1.20 mm max height, 20 pins

Step by step

  1. Family – 1.27 mm pitch parts use the fixed prefix SOIC127P. All other pitches (SSOP, TSSOP, MSOP) use SOP + pitch.
  2. Lead span – E, lead tip to lead tip, nominal: 5.80–6.20 → 6.00 → 600.
  3. Height – A maximum: 1.75 → 175.
  4. Pin count and density – -8N.

Common mistakes

E1 instead of EA narrow SOIC body (E1) is about 3.9 mm, the lead span (E) about 6.0 mm. Using 3.90 gives SOIC127P390…, which describes a different footprint.
Nominal heightUse A max, not the typical value.
Wide-body partsWide SOIC-16 has a lead span near 10.3 mm: SOIC127P1030X265-16N. Same pin count, different name – that is the point.
